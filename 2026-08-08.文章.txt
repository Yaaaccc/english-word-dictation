A School Excursion to the Mountains

Last weekend, our class went on a school excursion to the northwestern mountains. The bus was old, and its bumper was covered with mud, but nobody cared. We were all active and happy. Our guide was a clever and experienced man. He was authoritative but kind, and he knew the local language well. An interpreter came with us too, because a few students could not understand every word.

The first morning, we walked to a huge cave. We saw a young seedling growing near the entrance, and only a few of us went inside. The cave was dark and cool. The guide asked for our consent before we went deeper, because it was illegal to enter the deepest part without permission. Inside, we saw a fish hide behind a rock. It tried to sting me with its tail, but I hid in time. The guide laughed and said the fish was harmless.

That afternoon we went fishing by the river. We used small pieces of bread as bait. One boy fell and hurt his mouth, and the nurse put a bandage on it. Later, at the small clinic, the doctor checked him carefully. He said the illness was not serious, and the boy did not fall into a coma. He cleaned the wound to avoid contamination and gave him a fresh bandage. The doctor also said that good health may depend on rest and exercise, and his words were truthful and clear.

At dinner, we ate soup from a big bowl and had a cookie for dessert. The price of the trip was inclusive of food, so we did not pay extra. A trainee and a typist from the school office joined us for the meal. One old man by the road was a drunkard, and he told us that lying is a sin. Nobody believed the rumour he spread about us. One boy was mad about it, but nevertheless we stayed calm.

At night, the moon was bright. I saw a similarity between the two hills near the camp. A cold wind caused a sudden contraction in my leg, so I did more exercise the next day. We also met a clever planner from the hotel. He was not manipulative, and his answer to our question was an implicit yes. The hotel personnel were friendly, and the price of a room was inclusive of breakfast. We cut our bread into a cube and ate it with honey. One seller tried to sell us too much food, but we bought only a small bundle. I saved the remainder for my family, and my sister loved the red stocking I bought her.

It was not a perfect trip. One loud speaker was disruptive during the guide's talk, and a drunkard slept by the road. Nevertheless, everyone agreed that it was a wonderful excursion. I was sad to leave, but it is probable that we will come again next year.
